1. Platform Governance & Service Overview

Escrown provides a peer-to-peer transaction platform with chat, transaction records, dispute reporting, and administrative review tools. Any real-money escrow holding or payout service requires a connected payment provider and server-side verification. By creating an account or initiating an escrow agreement on our platform, you agree to comply with all terms stated herein.

2. Service Commission & Fees

To ensure high platform availability, end-to-end security, and dispute verification mechanisms, Escrown charges a service commission fee:

  • Commission Rate: The current application calculates a 2% service fee on the agreed transaction amount.
  • Fee Assessment: The 2% fee is calculated and displayed when the parties record an agreement.
  • Non-Refundable Administrative Charges: Upon successful transaction completion and payout settlement, platform commission fees are non-refundable.

3. Escrow Holds & Payout Settlement

The application records transaction states, but it does not claim that funds are held, verified, or paid out until a real payment provider/webhook and server-side release process has been configured. Administrative actions are protected by server-side authorization.

4. Transaction Flagging & Dispute Resolution

Users possess the right to invoke the /flagtransaction function for active transactions in event of breach, fraud, or non-delivery. Flagging a transaction routes the dispute ID directly to platform governance for manual verification through the administrative review workflow. Initiating a new transaction seals and finalizes previous transaction IDs from retrospective dispute claims.

5. Prohibited Conduct

Users are strictly prohibited from utilizing Escrown for illicit trade, fraud, money laundering, or bypass attempt of platform governance. Violations will result in immediate account termination and permanent blacklisting of associated account credentials.
