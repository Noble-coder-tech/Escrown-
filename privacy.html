Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To maintain secure transaction integrity, Escrown collects basic user credentials and transaction metadata during platform interaction:

  • Account Identification: Display name, email address, assigned Escrown ID, and payment account details.
  • Chat Data & Logs: Real-time messaging exchanges, contract agreements, transaction states, and timestamp histories required for dispute resolution.
  • Administrative Incident Data: Email communications and flagged transaction records dispatched during support escalations.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information gathered by Escrown is deployed strictly for operational performance:

  • Facilitating automated contract generation via Escrown escrow logic.
  • Verifying settlement payout details and administrative ledger accounting.
  • Contacting users directly via registered email addresses during active transaction disputes.

3. Data Protection & Session Security

User authentication state is governed using strict session persistence parameters, automatically terminating authorization upon window or browser closure. We do not sell, publicize, or rent user credentials to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Retention

Transaction records and completed ledger entries are preserved to maintain operational logs, mitigate fraud risks, and provide verifiable proof of settlement payouts.
